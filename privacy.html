Privacy Policy

Life OS · personal automation tooling · last updated 30 October 2026

What this covers

Life OS is a private command-line tool that Nishant Tomer runs on his own computer to organise his coursework, calendar, tasks and email. It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to anyone else. This policy describes what it does with Google account data, because Google requires a published policy for any application that requests OAuth access.

Who uses it

One person: the developer, Nishant Tomer. The application has no other users, no sign-up, no accounts and no shared backend. There is no server. Everything runs locally.

What data it accesses

With the developer's own explicit Google authorisation, the tool may read:

  • Google Classroom coursework, submission states and announcements
  • Google Calendar events, to show upcoming deadlines
  • Gmail message headers and bodies, to summarise unread mail
  • Google Drive and Sheets files, to read documents the developer points it at
  • Google Tasks items

What it does with that data

  • It is read, summarised, and written to plain text files on the developer's own machine.
  • It is not transmitted to any third party.
  • It is not sold, rented, shared, published or used for advertising.
  • It is not used to train any model.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry or tracking of any kind on this data.

Where credentials live

OAuth tokens are stored locally under the developer's home directory (~/.config/gws/), protected by the operating system user account. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Retention and deletion

Generated summaries live in local files and are overwritten on each run. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the application's access.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
